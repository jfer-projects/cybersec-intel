🔴 FortiMail Zero-Day Under Active Exploitation — Unauthenticated Arbitrary File Write

🆕 CVE-2026-104286 (CVSS 9.8): Path Traversal and Null-Byte Flaw in Fortinet FortiMail, Added to CISA KEV October 1, 2026 (reported October 2)
Fortinet disclosed a critical flaw combining path traversal and improper null-byte neutralization that lets an unauthenticated attacker write arbitrary files on the underlying system through crafted HTTP/HTTPS requests. Affected: FortiMail 8.0.0–8.0.1, 7.6.0–7.6.6, 7.4.0–7.4.8 and 7.2.0–7.2.9. In-the-wild exploitation is confirmed; published indicators include IPs 79.141.169.187 and 45.129.0.192 and modified or added system files. CISA added the CVE to KEV on October 1 with a federal deadline of October 4. Patches were not yet available for all versions at the time of reporting; Fortinet’s workarounds are disabling the IBE (identity-based encryption) feature and restricting management-interface access to trusted networks. No threat actor has been attributed.
PORTFOLIO IMPACT: FortiMail is a common email-security gateway in MSP-managed SMB environments, and email is the primary phishing channel our SAT and EDR/MDR products defend against. Arbitrary file write on a mail gateway can lead to web shells and lateral movement. MSP customers running FortiMail should apply the workarounds immediately, hunt for the listed IOCs and unexpected file changes, and apply fixed builds as soon as Fortinet releases them. Competitive note: Fortinet is also an MDR/endpoint competitor.

🟡 Third NetScaler Flaw in Two Weeks — SAML Memory Overflow Exploited for Denial of Service

🆕 CVE-2026-88779 (CVSS 8.7): NetScaler ADC/Gateway SAML Memory Overflow, Added to KEV (October 5, 2026)
Citrix patched CVE-2026-88779, a memory overflow affecting NetScaler ADC/Gateway when configured as a SAML service provider or identity provider (builds 14.1-73.41 and 13.1-64.28 and later, plus the corresponding FIPS/NDcPP builds). Citrix has observed targeted attacks on unmitigated deployments that cause denial of service. Unlike last week’s CVE-2026-88771/88772 (exploited to deploy web shells and tunneling tools), this flaw causes service disruption only, with no identified impact on customer data integrity. CISA added it to KEV with a federal deadline of October 7.
PORTFOLIO IMPACT: Customers who already patched for 88771/88772 should confirm they are on the newest builds, since SAML-fronted remote access outages affect every user behind the gateway. Lower severity than last week’s pair, but it extends the run of NetScaler exploitation reported in the September 28 briefing.

🟡 Operation KillSwitch — Law Enforcement Seizes KillSec Ransomware Infrastructure

🆕 Servers and Domains Seized, Three Arrests (September 30, 2026)
Per a weekly roundup, authorities seized KillSec ransomware infrastructure on September 30 — five servers and multiple domains, with more than 110 TB of data secured — and made three arrests, including a 16-year-old identified as the lead administrator. The group is linked to roughly 1,000 suspected attacks, about 500 believed successful. This item rests on a single aggregator source; confirmation from official law-enforcement statements was not located at the time of writing.
PORTFOLIO IMPACT: A takedown can temporarily reduce activity, but affiliates typically rebrand or migrate. MDR and EDR detection content for KillSec tooling should stay in place; the seized data may produce victim notifications in the coming weeks.
