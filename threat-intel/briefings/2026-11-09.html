🔴 Actively Exploited: Network Security Appliances

🆕 CVE-2026-19868: SonicWall SonicOS vulnerability exploited in the wild
The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (advisory AV26-1133, November 6) relays that SonicWall indicates CVE-2026-19868 is being exploited; the same page states CISA added it to the KEV catalog on November 6. Affected: SonicOS prior to 7.3.4-7013 and prior to 8.2.2-8015 (interpreted as the fixed versions). CVSS, the vulnerability class, indicators and threat-actor attribution were not available in the source read.
PORTFOLIO IMPACT: SonicWall firewalls are widespread in MSP-managed SMB environments. Inventory SonicOS versions now, upgrade to fixed builds, restrict management-plane exposure, and confirm firewall logs are reaching MDR/SIEM. Verify details against SonicWall's PSIRT advisory and CISA's KEV entry before customer communications.
