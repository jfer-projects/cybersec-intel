🆕 KnowBe4 Names Kurt Mills as Channel Chief to Lead Next Phase of Partner-Led Growth (September 30, 2026)
🏷 KnowBe4  |  📅 September 30, 2026
KnowBe4 appointed Kurt Mills as channel chief. Mills has roughly 25 years of channel leadership at Trellix, Check Point Software, Mimecast, FireMon and Blue Coat, and a record of scaling partner networks through corporate transitions. His stated mandate is to "scale our partner programs" and deepen relationships across VARs, MSPs, SIs, distributors and technology partners. The hire follows the recent appointments of Neill Burton and John Noha as vice presidents of channel. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: KnowBe4 is investing in a dedicated, senior channel organization — MSPs are named explicitly — at the same time it is moving into real-time shadow-AI enforcement (Agent Risk Manager, reported Sept 28). Expect more partner incentives and MSP-oriented packaging. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth reviewing how our SAT offering's MSP economics, multi-tenant management and bundling with EP/EDR/MDR/DNS compare as KnowBe4 courts the same partners. No program or pricing details were disclosed in the announcement.
