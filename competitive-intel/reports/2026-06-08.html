🌐 DNS Protection & Threat Intelligence (1 new item)

🆕 Infoblox Completes Axur Acquisition + Launches Digital Risk Protection Services (May 5, 2026)
🏷 Infoblox & Axur  |  📅 May 5, 2026
Infoblox completed acquisition of Axur, a Brazilian AI-powered external threat discovery platform. Axur brings continuous monitoring of open web, social platforms, mobile apps, and dark web for threats. Infoblox launched Digital Risk Protection Services (DRPS): monitors 40M+ URLs daily using multi-modal AI to detect/validate phishing, brand abuse, executive impersonation, credential exposure. Automated takedown orchestration at scale. IMPACT: Consolidation of external threat intelligence + DNS security. Infoblox positioning DNS as foundational infrastructure for both internal (DNS queries) + external (threat landscape) visibility. Competitive implication: DNS vendors expanding threat intelligence scope. Differentiation window: external threat monitoring + DNS blocking becoming expected baseline. Sales narrative: "DNS-native preemptive security: stop threats before they reach your endpoints." Threat to Cisco: Infoblox using external threat discovery to offset Cisco's installed base in DNS filtering.

📌 Strategic Context: Scope Expansion in EDR + Threat Intel

Two clear trends emerging Q2 2026: (1) EDR platforms expanding beyond endpoints → AI tool governance (CrowdStrike Claude integration). Implication: Detection scope broadening; single-purpose point solutions losing appeal. (2) Threat intelligence + external threat discovery consolidating. Infoblox's Axur acquisition signals DNS vendors extending into preemptive external threat detection. Competitive window: Vendors offering integrated endpoint + AI + external threat detection gaining advantage. Portfolio implication: Emphasize cross-platform threat detection (endpoint + AI + external) + autonomous response. Bundled platform advantage strengthening.
