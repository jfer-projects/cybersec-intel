🌐 DNS Protection (2 new items)

🆕 Cisco Umbrella End-of-Life: Migration to Cisco Secure Access (May 2026)
🏷 Cisco Documentation  |  📅 May 7 & 15, 2026
Cisco announced formal end-of-life for legacy Umbrella SKUs. Key dates: Support email addresses decommissioned (May 7); certificate transition from DigiCert to Identrust (May 15). Software maintenance releases end September 30, 2026; active TAC support continues through September 30, 2030. Organizations must migrate to Cisco Secure Access (rebranded SASE solution) or evaluate vendor-neutral alternatives. IMPACT: Major DNS market consolidation trigger. Infoblox, Quad9, and third-party vendors will aggressively acquire Cisco customers. Timeline: POC through June 2026, procurement decision June-Aug, migration completion by Sept 30, 2026. Competitive opportunity window: now through August for alternative vendors.
🆕 Infoblox Competitive Promotion: 15 Months for 12 (Through July 31, 2026)
🏷 Infoblox  |  📅 May 2026
Infoblox launched time-limited promotional offer: 15 months of Infoblox Threat Defense for the cost of 12 months. Offer valid through July 31, 2026. Explicitly targets Cisco Umbrella migration customers. IMPACT: Signals competitive intensity around DNS market consolidation. Infoblox capitalizing on Cisco's strategic retreat to acquire high-value customers during migration window. Validates bundled threat intelligence strategy (per May 18 Axur acquisition). Pricing pressure signal for competitive positioning.

🛡️ Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) (1 new item)

🆕 Microsoft Defender for Endpoint: Automatic Attack Disruption Feature (May 18)
🏷 Microsoft  |  📅 May 18, 2026
Microsoft Defender for Endpoint released new Public Preview feature allowing users to view real-time status of automatic attack disruption and predictive shielding actions linked to specific incidents. Automatic device isolation enhancements enabled as of May 18. Feature provides incident visibility into containment actions. IMPACT: Continues feature parity expansion against CrowdStrike Falcon and SentinelOne Singularity. Microsoft's bundled EDR advantage strengthens with M365 integration + automatic response capabilities. Competitive implication: standalone EDR vendors must differentiate on behavioral detection + kernel-level protection (per May 18 GhostLocker threat briefing).

📌 Strategic Context: DNS Market Consolidation Accelerating

Cisco Umbrella EOL signals major DNS vendor consolidation. Infoblox (Axur acquisition + promotional offer), Quad9, and alternative vendors will compete aggressively for migrating customers through Q3 2026. Combined with Infoblox/Axur threat intelligence bundling (May 5), DNS market shifting from point solution dominance to integrated threat intelligence + DNS platform bundles. Implication: Any DNS-adjacent positioning should emphasize bundled threat intelligence value + migration support through 2026.
