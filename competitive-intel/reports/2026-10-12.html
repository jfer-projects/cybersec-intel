🆕 CrowdStrike Named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Modern Endpoint Security for Enterprises (October 7, 2026)
🏷 CrowdStrike  |  📅 October 7, 2026
CrowdStrike announced it was named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Modern Endpoint Security for Enterprises 2026. IDC cited "deep AI and agentic investment" tied to reported SOC efficiency gains, and the breadth of the platform across endpoint, identity, cloud, data, exposure management, browser and mobile. In the same release CrowdStrike said its AI Detection and Response (AIDR) business has grown more than 79-fold in three quarters since launch (citing its Fal.Con 2026 Investor Day) and promoted Falcon Guardian for securing AI agents at the point of execution; CPO AJ Shipley says CrowdStrike "pioneered this market." COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: This builds on the SafeMind / OpenAI partnership reported September 7 and reinforces a "secure the agents" positioning that rivals (Microsoft, SentinelOne, KnowBe4) are also pursuing. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: The MarketScape targets enterprises, but MSPs increasingly cite analyst placement when choosing a stack. Consider whether our EP/EDR/MDR messaging has independent validation (analyst, MITRE, AV-Comparatives) to counter it. Growth figure is vendor-reported with no absolute base disclosed.
