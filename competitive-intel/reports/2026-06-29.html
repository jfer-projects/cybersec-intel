PORTFOLIO ANGLE: The deepfake and AI agent attack surface is rapidly materializing — MSPs with clients deploying Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Einstein, or other AI agents face real prompt injection and social engineering risks that traditional SAT does not address. This creates a genuine product gap conversation to watch.

🛡️ Endpoint Protection / EDR — Supply Chain Ransomware Context (1 item)

🔴 World Leaks Ransomware Breaches Tata Electronics — 200K+ Files Including Apple, TSMC, Qualcomm, Tesla IP Leaked (June 23–26, 2026)
🏷 World Leaks Ransomware Group / Tata Electronics  |  📅 June 23–26, 2026
World Leaks, a ransomware group, breached Tata Electronics — a major Indian electronics manufacturer that is a key Apple supplier and a central part of Apple's strategy to shift iPhone assembly to India. World Leaks posted over 200,000 files to the dark web following an apparent ransomware deployment, including: Apple Silicon Engineering Group documents mapping Apple parts numbers to TSMC components; TSMC documents marked "TSMC Secret" containing product reliability test data; documents linked to Qualcomm and Tesla. Tata Electronics responded by restricting internal access to affected systems, hiring a global forensic consultant, and reporting the incident to the Indian government and clients. Apple dispatched its security team to support the investigation. Reuters was unable to authenticate all leaked documents, but Apple confirmed awareness and investigation. World Leaks is a relatively new threat group; this represents their most high-profile victim to date. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Tata Electronics is a large-scale, globally significant manufacturer — exactly the type of complex industrial environment where EP/EDR deployments are often inconsistent, legacy, or absent on production systems. The breach reinforces that ransomware groups are prioritizing supply chain IP theft over immediate operational disruption. This is a meaningful competitive differentiator conversation: does your endpoint protection cover OT/manufacturing environments, not just IT? PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Manufacturing and industrial supply chains represent a growing untapped MDR/EP opportunity where endpoint hygiene is weakest. Mistic backdoor activity (see Threat Intel) uses similar DLL side-loading techniques targeting the same organizational profile. For MSPs managing manufacturing clients, the risk posture conversation is now urgent.

📌 Strategic Context: AI-Augmented HRM + Supply Chain EP Gap

Two signals this week reinforce distinct but complementary themes. In SAT/HRM, KnowBe4's sixth consecutive G2 #1 and its AI-Native Summit demonstrate that the category is splitting: traditional SAT (phishing simulations, compliance training) remains dominated by KnowBe4 by a wide margin, while a new "AI agent risk" category is forming around deepfake detection, AI behavior governance, and agentic identity — where the competitive landscape is open. In endpoint/EDR, the Tata Electronics breach by World Leaks illustrates that global supply chains extending into manufacturing environments are high-value ransomware targets with systematically weaker endpoint postures. MSPs serving any manufacturing or industrial clients should treat this as an active risk conversation.
