🆕 ESET NeoCortex security model powers LiveCortex incident correlation in ESET PROTECT
🏷 ESET  |  📅 October 22, 2026 (blog); aggregator write-up November 3
ESET described NeoCortex, a proprietary model combining language-model capabilities with a world-model approach focused on security incidents. It powers LiveCortex, a correlation engine in ESET PROTECT that groups weak signals into incidents, prioritizes them and responds or hands off to a human. ESET says it uses read-only tools, sandboxes, a kill switch and human-in-the-loop for critical decisions, runs on far less compute than general reasoning models, and can be self-hosted for sovereignty-sensitive customers. ESET cites an internal funnel of roughly 1 in 90,000 telemetry events becoming a real incident. It states LiveCortex is not a SIEM replacement; no benchmarks, GA date, pricing or tier mapping were given. An "incident sensitivity control" usable by MSPs and "minus-one-day" attack-step prediction are described as future direction, not shipped. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: ESET (see October 19 AI Security module) is building AI differentiation at the incident layer, not just agent governance. If our EP/EDR stack has no incident-correlation story for MSP analysts, this is the claim to benchmark against; watch for MSP tuning controls and tier packaging.
Sources: ESET blog · IndustryLens (aggregator)

🎯 MDR (1 new item)

🆕 Rapid7 Q3 2026: ARR and revenue decline, restructuring, pivot to AI-powered managed operations
🏷 Rapid7  |  📅 November 4, 2026 (Form 8-K)
Rapid7 reported Q3 ARR of $819.7M (-2.2% YoY) and total revenue of $212.4M (-2.5%), with product subscription revenue of $206.1M (-1.9%). Non-GAAP operating income rose to $46.2M (from $36.9M) and free cash flow was $26.5M, helped by a $10.0M restructuring charge in the quarter and a $5.5M impairment of a strategic investment. Guidance: Q4 ARR about $812M (about -3% YoY), Q4 revenue $208–210M, FY2026 revenue $841–843M. The company calls itself a leader in AI-powered managed cybersecurity operations; October highlights include Rapid7 Intelligence (unified threat intel, vulnerability research and Labs, led by Mel Stone) and a preview of an Autonomous SOC (experts plus AI agents). About 11,800 customers; the release breaks out no MDR-specific metrics. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Rapid7 is cutting costs and shrinking while leaning harder into managed services, so expect sharper MDR pricing and bundling in the mid-market. For MSP-focused MDR, treat Rapid7 customers and partners as a conversion opportunity, but note its AI/autonomous-SOC messaging will mirror Arctic Wolf and SentinelOne.
Sources: Rapid7 Q3 2026 8-K Exhibit 99.1
