🌐 DNS Protection (2 new items)

🆕 Quad9 DNSSEC Strict Validation Enabled (June 15, 2026)
🏷 Quad9  |  📅 June 1 Announcement, June 15 Activation
Quad9 enabling DNSSEC validation on remaining non-validating resolver address; DNSSEC strict validation active across every Quad9 service endpoint starting June 15, 2026. IMPACT: Cryptographic DNS integrity now baseline security posture. Quad9 (Swiss non-profit, no-logging, DNSSEC-validated) competitive positioning strengthens vs. commercial DNS vendors lacking DNSSEC enforcement. Implication: Threat intelligence solutions must account for DNSSEC validation as detection baseline; DNS as trust infrastructure centerpiece elevated.
🆕 Infoblox + GoDaddy AI Agent Identification Standards (May 2026)
🏷 Infoblox & GoDaddy  |  📅 May 2026
Infoblox and GoDaddy jointly announcing two open DNS standards: DNS for AI Discovery (DNS-AID) and Agent Name Service (ANS). Enable AI agents to identify, locate, and verify themselves on the open web using DNS infrastructure. IMPACT: DNS evolving beyond threat detection → AI agent discovery and attribution. Infoblox positioning DNS as foundational AI security control. Strategic implication: Threat intelligence solutions must integrate AI agent behavior patterns; DNS-native AI security emerging as competitive category.
