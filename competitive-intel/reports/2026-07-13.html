🎯 Managed Detection & Response (1 item)

🆕 Huntress Signs Giacom + MSP Nordics Distribution Deals, Expands Microsoft Defender Integration (July 7–8, 2026)
🏷 Huntress  |  📅 July 7–8, 2026
Huntress announced two distribution partnerships in the same week: one with UK channel marketplace Giacom, giving MSPs on Giacom's Cloud Market platform direct access to Huntress' Agentic Security Platform and 24/7 AI-centric SOC; and a parallel agreement with MSP Nordics covering Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, and Sweden. Combined, the deals extend MDR, ITDR, and managed SIEM access to roughly 6,000 MSPs across the UK and Nordics. Separately, Huntress expanded its Microsoft collaboration to connect its tools directly into Defender and Microsoft 365 Business Premium — targeted at businesses that already run the Microsoft stack but lack the staff to fully operationalize it. Huntress also took Managed Identity Security Posture Management (ISPM) to general availability (continuously hardening Microsoft 365 configurations) and shipped AI Search for Managed SIEM, letting analysts query logs in plain English instead of ESQL or a query builder. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Huntress is executing on two fronts simultaneously that matter most to MSP buyers — channel distribution reach and native depth on the Microsoft 365/Defender stack most SMB clients already run. The AI Search and ISPM GA releases also reduce the operational burden on lean MSP SOC teams, a differentiator that resonates directly with our own purpose-built-for-MSP positioning. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Huntress remains the closest MDR competitor to track for feature parity and channel reach; the EMEA distribution expansion (6,000 MSPs) and Microsoft-native integration depth are the two most important threads to monitor going into Q3 planning.

🌐 DNS Protection (1 item)

🆕 Infoblox to Acquire Kentik — Bundling DNS Threat Intelligence with Network Observability (July 8, 2026)
🏷 Infoblox / Kentik  |  📅 July 8, 2026
Infoblox entered a definitive agreement to acquire Kentik, a network intelligence and observability platform, uniting Infoblox's authoritative DNS and preemptive, DNS-based threat intelligence with Kentik's network flow data. Per Infoblox CEO Scott Harrell, the combined platform is intended to give networking, cloud, and security teams real-time hybrid-cloud intelligence, compressing triage time by correlating DNS-layer threat signals with asset connections and data-volume anomalies detected via flow data. This is Infoblox's second notable acquisition this year, following the May 18 Axur deal. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Infoblox is using its position as the primary beneficiary of Cisco Umbrella's standalone-DNS wind-down to build out a broader network-visibility platform rather than staying a DNS-only point solution. This raises the long-term bar for what "DNS protection" competitively needs to include — bundled network observability, not just domain filtering and threat-intel freshness. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Two Infoblox acquisitions in under two months (Axur in May, Kentik in July) signal an aggressive platform-expansion strategy while the Cisco Umbrella migration window remains open. DNS protection positioning conversations should increasingly account for adjacent visibility/observability capability, not just detection-rate benchmarks.

🆕 KnowBe4 2026 Phishing by Industry Benchmarking Report: 79% Susceptibility Drop After One Year of Training (July 7, 2026)
🏷 KnowBe4  |  📅 July 7, 2026
KnowBe4 published its 2026 Phishing by Industry Benchmarking Report, analyzing 42 million phishing simulations across 14.8 million users at 64,000 organizations worldwide. Headline finding: organizations that ran consistent security awareness training for one full year saw a 79% reduction in phishing susceptibility. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Large-sample, vendor-published efficacy data like this becomes a reference point in SAT/HRM sales conversations industry-wide, whether or not the prospect is evaluating KnowBe4 specifically — buyers will ask "can you show similar numbers?" PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Useful external benchmark to cite (or beat) in SAT ROI conversations; also worth checking whether our own phishing-simulation reporting can produce a comparable industry-benchmarked efficacy figure for sales enablement.

📌 Strategic Context: Platform Consolidation Is the Week's Common Thread

Every item this week points toward the same underlying dynamic: point-solution vendors are bundling adjacent capability to defend or expand share. Huntress is bundling channel reach (Giacom, MSP Nordics) with Microsoft-native depth (Defender/M365 integration, ISPM, AI Search) rather than competing on MDR detection alone. Infoblox is bundling DNS threat intelligence with network observability (Kentik, following May's Axur deal) rather than staying a DNS-only play. CrowdStrike's growth is increasingly an AI-native detection and response story rather than a traditional EDR one. Even KnowBe4's CTO promotion reinforces continuity on its "Human + Agent Security" bundling strategy flagged in the June 29 report. PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION: Point-solution positioning within any single category (MDR-only, DNS-only, EDR-only) is getting harder to defend against vendors bundling adjacent capability — worth a explicit discussion of where our six-product portfolio's cross-sell story can be sharpened to match this trend, particularly MDR + DNS + SAT bundling for MSP buyers who increasingly expect single-vendor coverage.
