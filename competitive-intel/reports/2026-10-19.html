🛡️ Endpoint Protection / 🔍 EDR (5 new items)

🆕 Sophos CISO Advantage (October 2026)
🏷 Sophos  |  📅 Early October 2026
Sophos launched CISO Advantage as an add-on to Sophos Fusion. It uses AI to assess vulnerabilities, plan risk reduction and track mitigation progress, and is aimed at helping MSPs tie compliance frameworks (NIST CSF, CIS Controls v8 per the roundup) to business outcomes. Routes remediation through partners. Available in Europe and North America; pricing undisclosed. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Sophos is competing for the MSP's advisory/vCISO layer, not just the agent. If our MSPs sell compliance reporting, expect Sophos to bundle it with EP/MDR.
Sources: Channel Dive · Industry Lens roundup (aggregator — verify against vendor releases)
🆕 ESET PROTECT portfolio (Core / Premium / Ultimate) + AI Security module
🏷 ESET  |  📅 September 30 – October 5, 2026
ESET introduced a three-tier PROTECT portfolio, initially in the US, Italy and Slovakia, and on October 5 added an AI Security module. Single-source (aggregator); tier contents and pricing not verified. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Good-better-best SMB packaging with AI governance as the top-tier differentiator.
🆕 Bitdefender AI Guardian and WithSecure Elements AI Security
🏷 Bitdefender, WithSecure  |  📅 October 7, 2026
Bitdefender released AI Guardian, a standalone product (free open beta on macOS) governing AI-agent actions on tools, files and credentials. WithSecure launched Elements AI Security to surface shadow AI tools, browser extensions and agentic identities for MSPs and mid-market teams. Both single-source. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Shadow-AI visibility is now being sold specifically to MSPs; a gap if our EP/EDR lacks AI-app discovery.
🆕 CrowdStrike Falcon Data Security for SaaS GA; Fall '26 release
🏷 CrowdStrike  |  📅 October 5, 2026
Falcon Data Security for SaaS became generally available, extending data classification into Microsoft 365 SharePoint and OneDrive without an additional sensor. Complements the Fall '26 platform release (Falcon Guardian AIDR already covered October 12). Source aggregator notes internal inconsistencies; treat details as provisional.
🆕 Trend Micro, Kaspersky, Acronis (minor)
🏷 Trend Micro, Kaspersky, Acronis  |  📅 October 5–6, 2026
Trend Micro opened closed preview of Autonomous Vulnerability Discovery in Vision One; Kaspersky added AI summarization and MCP support to its Threat Intelligence Portal (relevant to BrightCloud positioning); Acronis shipped Cyber Protect v17 Q3 2026. Single-source.
