🎯 Managed Detection & Response (2 new items)

🆕 Arctic Wolf Launches Aurora MDR Connect, an MSP-Exclusive MDR Tier With No Network Sensor Required (September 15, 2026)
🏷 Arctic Wolf  |  📅 September 15, 2026
Arctic Wolf launched Aurora MDR Connect, a new tier of its Aurora MDR service built exclusively for managed service providers. Powered by the Aurora Superintelligence Platform and Aurora Agentic SOC, it delivers 24x7 detection, investigation, and pre-authorized response with no network sensor to ship, rack, or configure — MSPs can deploy across customer environments within hours. Arctic Wolf says the underlying platform now processes more than 10 trillion security events and conducts over 200,000 investigations weekly across an ecosystem of 250+ integrations. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: This is Arctic Wolf's second MSP-facing packaging move in six weeks, following the August 3 Cyber Resilience warranty bundle — the company is iterating quickly on both pricing/risk-transfer (bundle + warranty) and deployment friction (sensor-free, hours-not-days) as competitive levers simultaneously. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth benchmarking our own MDR time-to-protect and deployment complexity against Arctic Wolf's "no sensor, hours not days" claim — deployment speed is emerging as its own competitive axis independent of detection quality.
🆕 Huntress Expands Into Africa Through a QBS Software Distribution Partnership (September 16, 2026)
🏷 Huntress  |  📅 September 16, 2026
Huntress established a distribution partnership with QBS Software Africa, giving MSPs and VARs across the continent access to its managed EDR/MDR platform and 24/7 SOC, with the initial rollout focused on South Africa ahead of a planned expansion into other Sub-Saharan markets. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: This continues Huntress's pattern of channel-led geographic expansion throughout 2026 rather than feature-led competition — the company is racing to establish distribution footprint in underserved MSP markets before competitors arrive. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Geographic MSP-channel coverage is becoming a competitive dimension in its own right; worth a quick check on whether our own distribution partnerships cover the same underserved regions Huntress is now entering.

🌐 DNS Protection (1 new item)

🆕 Cisco Sets Formal End-of-Sale and End-of-Life Dates for Umbrella SIG and DNS, Pushing Customers to Secure Access (September 17, 2026)
🏷 Cisco  |  📅 September 17, 2026
Cisco published a formal end-of-sale and end-of-life notice for Umbrella SIG and DNS: end-of-sale is January 31, 2027; existing subscriptions can be renewed only until January 31, 2028; support continues until January 31, 2029; and no new software maintenance releases will ship after September 30, 2026. Cisco is directing all affected customers to migrate to Secure Access – DNS Defense, its successor product. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Earlier reports flagged Infoblox running informal switch-from-Cisco promotions against Secure Access's disputed 39.3% threat-block-rate testing (July 20 report) — this notice converts that soft migration pressure into a hard, dated deadline that every remaining Umbrella DNS customer must now act on. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: A forced migration with a public deadline is a rare, concrete opening for DNS Protection outreach — worth a dated campaign targeting Umbrella SIG/DNS customers before the September 30 software-maintenance cutoff pushes them toward Cisco's own upgrade path by default.

🎓 Security Awareness Training / HRM (1 new item)

🆕 Hoxhunt Expands AI-Native SAT Platform With Instant Custom Content and Interactive Voice Training (September 15, 2026)
🏷 Hoxhunt  |  📅 September 15, 2026
Hoxhunt expanded its Security Awareness Training platform with company-specific AI-generated content produced in seconds, broader topic coverage, faster localization, automated administration, and interactive AI voice training that gives employees realistic practice against social-engineering attempts that go beyond email, such as vishing. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Hoxhunt is pressing its AI-native content-generation speed as a direct challenge to KnowBe4's ModStore-and-AIDA approach (September 7 area context), and the voice-training piece specifically targets a growing attack vector — voice phishing — that most SAT libraries still treat as a secondary scenario rather than a dedicated training track. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth checking whether our own SAT/HRM content roadmap has a dedicated vishing/voice-based social-engineering module, since two competitors (Hoxhunt here, KnowBe4's AIDA agents previously) are now building distinct capabilities around it.

📌 Strategic Context: Three Different Categories, One Shared Pattern — Reduce Friction to Win the MSP Channel

This week's items span MDR, DNS, and SAT, but each one reduces a different kind of friction for the MSP buyer: Arctic Wolf removes deployment friction (no sensor, hours not days), Huntress removes distribution friction (new regional partner network), Cisco is about to remove customer inertia by force (a hard EOL deadline), and Hoxhunt removes content-creation friction (instant custom training, including voice). None of these are pure product feature wins — they're all about making it easier or more urgent for an MSP or SMB buyer to act. PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION: Worth a short cross-functional note comparing our own MDR onboarding time, channel geographic coverage, and SAT content-authoring speed against this week's moves — the competitive question increasingly isn't "whose detection is better" but "who is easiest and fastest to buy from and deploy."
