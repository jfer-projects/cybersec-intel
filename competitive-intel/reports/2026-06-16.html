🛡️ Endpoint Protection (1 new item)

🆕 Sophos Backup and Recovery Powered by Rubrik: M365 Resilience Inside Sophos Central (June 1, 2026)
🏷 Sophos & Rubrik  |  📅 June 1, 2026
Sophos and Rubrik made generally available Sophos Backup and Recovery powered by Rubrik Cyber Resilience — native immutable backup and rapid recovery for Microsoft 365 Exchange Online and OneDrive, managed within Sophos Central. Follows a strategic partnership announced August 2025. Immutable backup enables rapid recovery after ransomware, account compromise, insider threats, or accidental deletion. Launching in US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia, and Japan. SharePoint Online, Microsoft Teams, and Planner support expected Q3 2026. IMPACT: Sophos bundling endpoint protection + immutable backup in one console. For MSP channel: reduces the tool stack (fewer separate backup vendors required). Competitive implication: Endpoint vendors bundling backup/recovery as a differentiator — raises the bar for competitors relying on third-party integrations. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Sophos Central now competes with both endpoint-only vendors and backup vendors. Watch for customers asking "does your endpoint tool include ransomware recovery?" as a standard evaluation question.

🎓 Security Awareness Training / Human Risk Management (1 new item)

🆕 KnowBe4 Launches "Spot the Vish" — 35th Interactive Game Targeting Voice Phishing (June 9–12, 2026)
🏷 KnowBe4  |  📅 June 9–12, 2026
KnowBe4 released "Spot the Vish," its 35th interactive training game and first specifically targeting real-time vishing (voice phishing) scenarios. Users are placed in audio-driven scenarios requiring split-second decisions when confronted with attackers impersonating IT support or executives. Background: KnowBe4's 2025 Phishing Threat Trends Report Volume Six documented a 449% increase in vishing attacks. Game available to all KnowBe4 customers through the AI-enabled ModStore. Context: This builds on KnowBe4's May 2026 AIDA Orchestration launch (autonomous AI for HRM), extending the platform's training coverage from email phishing into voice channels. IMPACT: SAT/HRM platforms now expected to cover the full social engineering threat spectrum — email, SMS, voice, AI-generated deepfake calls. Gamified, multi-channel training becoming the competitive standard. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: KnowBe4 widening platform scope; single-channel SAT platforms (email only) increasingly at a disadvantage in deals where prospects benchmark multi-vector simulation coverage.

🔍 Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) (1 new item)

🆕 Microsoft Decouples Defender for Endpoint EDR Updates from Monthly Patch Cycle (June 8, 2026)
🏷 Microsoft  |  📅 June 8, 2026
Microsoft announced that Defender for Endpoint EDR sensor updates will now be distributed through Microsoft Update independently of monthly Windows OS cumulative updates (Patch Tuesday). Previously, EDR improvements were bundled into monthly Windows cumulative updates — meaning customers waited up to 30 days for detection enhancements. Rollout started for Windows 10 (late May 2026); Windows 11 and Server support rolling out through fall 2026. Updates will typically not require device restarts. IMPACT: Microsoft closes a significant competitive gap: CrowdStrike and SentinelOne have always delivered sensor updates continuously (cloud-native architecture). Now Microsoft can ship EDR fixes, detection improvements, and behavioral rules without waiting for the next Patch Tuesday release. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: For MSPs evaluating Defender for Endpoint vs. purpose-built EDR: the bundled cost advantage just got more defensible. Microsoft's EDR now approaches cloud-native update velocity. Competitive pressure on standalone EDR pricing and update-speed differentiation intensifies.

📌 Strategic Context: Platform Bundling Accelerates

Three signals this week converge on a single theme: incumbent endpoint vendors are bundling more capabilities into their core platforms, reducing the need for point solutions. Sophos now offers immutable backup inside its console. Microsoft decouples EDR updates from Patch Tuesday, matching cloud-native delivery speed. KnowBe4 expands SAT into multi-channel voice simulation, raising the bar on single-vector competitors. For an MSP-focused portfolio: this bundling trend reinforces platform consolidation advantage. Customers increasingly want to buy fewer, integrated tools — favor vendors who can cover more of the security stack without requiring third-party integrations.
