🎯 Managed Detection & Response (1 new item)

🆕 Fortinet Acquires Virtue AI to Strengthen AI Runtime Protection and Agent Governance (August 17, 2026)
🏷 Fortinet  |  📅 August 17, 2026
Fortinet acquired Virtue AI, whose Guardian Agent technology performs automated red-teaming of autonomous AI agents across more than 50 sandboxed environments and 14 risk domains, plus agent visibility/governance, continuous validation across model updates, and real-time guardrails spanning prompts, models, agents, Model Context Protocol tool calls, and API traffic. Terms were not disclosed; Fortinet called the deal immaterial to its financials. The technology folds into Fortinet's existing FortiAIGate line as part of a broader "Security for AI" strategy. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Fortinet — a named MDR competitor in our portfolio — is moving to own the "who's watching the AI agents" conversation before it becomes a standard RFP line item, positioning AI-agent security as a natural extension of its firewall/SASE/MDR stack rather than something MSPs need to source separately. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth a short internal check on whether our MDR/EDR roadmap has any planned coverage for AI agent/MCP-tool monitoring, since Fortinet, Palo Alto, and CrowdStrike (via recent SGNL/XM Cyber deals) are all now staking claims in adjacent AI-security ground.

🛡️ Endpoint Protection / EDR (1 new item)

🆕 Sophos Next-Gen SIEM Reaches General Availability, Priced by Users/Servers Not Data Volume (August 15, 2026)
🏷 Sophos  |  📅 August 15, 2026
Sophos Next-Gen SIEM — the data-retention and compliance layer of the Sophos Fusion platform announced in July — became a generally available paid add-on to Sophos XDR and Sophos MDR. It's priced by user and server count rather than by data volume, offers AI-assisted log parsers, flexible ingestion of non-Sophos telemetry, built-in compliance dashboards, and retention up to 10 years. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: Volume-based SIEM pricing is a known pain point for SMB/MSP clients who get surprise bills after a busy logging month; Sophos is explicitly marketing predictable, seat-based pricing as the alternative, which converts a technical feature (long retention) into a budget-certainty sales pitch. PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth flagging to sales as a likely talking point in competitive deals where Sophos is present — if our own MDR/EDR pricing is volume-sensitive, Sophos now has a specific, dated GA announcement to point to.

📌 Strategic Context: A Quiet Data Point Worth Keeping in the Back Pocket

Neither headline item this week is a direct feature-for-feature move against our specific products, but a research release from risk-rating vendor Black Kite (August 18, 2026) is worth noting for messaging purposes: analysis of over 13,000 ransomware incidents from 2023–2026 found that mid-market organizations ($10M–$1B revenue) account for roughly 73% of ransomware victims every year, with more than half of those victims under $50M in revenue — the exact buyer profile the MSP/SMB channel serves. Separately, a channel-trust survey published this week found that 33% of businesses considering switching MSPs cite "limited support for AI or automation" as a top driver, alongside reactive (rather than proactive) service. PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION: Both data points reinforce the same pitch for our six-product portfolio — mid-market/SMB is not a lower-priority ransomware target, it is the primary one, and MSPs are increasingly expected to pair strong security execution with visible AI/automation capability (the exact ground Fortinet's Virtue AI deal and Sophos's AI-assisted SIEM parsers are both staking out this week) to retain accounts.
