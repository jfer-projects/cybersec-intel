🆕 Microsoft Defender Experts for MDR P2 Reaches GA With Multi-Vendor Telemetry Ingestion (August 2026)
🏷 Microsoft  |  📅 August 2026
Microsoft's Defender Experts for MDR P2 service reached general availability with expanded third-party and multi-cloud coverage, ingesting and correlating telemetry from CrowdStrike (endpoint), AWS and Okta (cloud/identity), Proofpoint (email), and Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, Fortinet, and Zscaler (network) through Microsoft Sentinel — giving customers end-to-end MDR visibility across heterogeneous, multi-vendor environments rather than a Microsoft-only stack. COMPETITIVE IMPLICATION: This follows the same "manage everything the customer already owns" pitch that pure-play MDR vendors (Arctic Wolf, Huntress, Rapid7) built their category on, but backed by Microsoft's Sentinel data platform and named integration with a direct EDR competitor (CrowdStrike). PORTFOLIO ANGLE: Worth confirming our own MDR's third-party ingestion breadth is at parity with this named vendor list, since Microsoft is explicitly targeting mixed-stack MSP clients — the exact buyer profile our MDR/EDR lines compete for.

📌 Strategic Context: MDR Is Becoming a Fight Over Whose Console Sees Everything

This week's three MDR items point in a consistent direction: Microsoft is explicitly ingesting a rival EDR vendor's telemetry into its own MDR console, CrowdStrike is pairing analyst validation with channel expansion to widen its own MDR footprint, and Rapid7 is narrowing focus rather than expanding — three different responses to the same underlying pressure, which is that MDR value increasingly comes from being the single pane of glass across a customer's entire (often multi-vendor) stack rather than from any one product's detection quality alone. PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION: For our own MDR and EDR lines, the competitive question is shifting from "is our detection as good as CrowdStrike's or SentinelOne's" toward "can our MDR ingest and correlate telemetry from whatever EDR, firewall, and identity tools an MSP's clients already run" — worth a direct comparison of our third-party ingestion list against the Microsoft, Arctic Wolf, and CrowdStrike vendor lists referenced in the last two reports.
